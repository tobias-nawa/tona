# f16 GEMM second version, 2026-10-24, own measurement

## Power limit instead of compute units

bench/micro/gemm2.hip with 10 repetitions per variant (short loads with pauses): all variants 33–50 TFLOPS,
clock 1.1–2.5 GHz at 35–99 W. With 400 repetitions (sustained load): **2.6–2.8 GHz at 170–180 W**, limit
`power1_cap` 182 W. The 93 TFLOPS from measurements/2026-10-22-micro (pure WMMA, ~3.1 GHz) therefore do not hold under sustained load.
Short measurements underestimate; measurements in the prefill (sustained load) are what counts.

## GPU idle time in the prefill (rocprofv3, kernel timeline, 12,000 tokens)

| | Prefill window | GPU busy | Idle |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| before | 11.19 s | 9.51 s | 1.68 s |
| embedding/PLE of the next chunk on the CPU in parallel | **9.71 s** | 8.96 s | **0.74 s** |

The cause was the CPU: embedding and PLE per chunk (2,048 tokens) 40–210 ms, almost only random reads in the
28.8 GB PLE table (16 rows per token, does not quite fit in the page cache). Now a second thread computes the
next chunk into a second pinned buffer. The first chunk remains uncovered (for server requests usually the
only one). 24 instead of 6 threads for reading: slower (130–970 ms per chunk). Reading the table with direct I/O
would be the next attempt.

Remaining idle 0.74 s: in the MoE the counting on the CPU (k_scatter → gemm_q ~0.6 ms each, 0.16 s) and waiting for
copies of pinned experts (~0.12 s), otherwise single gaps of ~250 µs. NLL bit-identical (0.915200778).

## DeltaNet in the prefill: wave kernel instead of block with synchronization per token

Previously `k_dn_recur`: block per (V head, 32 columns), 8 waves share the 128 k rows, per token sums via LDS with
block synchronization (2.9 µs per token). New `k_dn_wave`: one wave per (V head, 16 columns), lane l holds k rows
4l … 4l + 3 (64 registers), sums by reduce-scatter via lane exchange, δ via readlane; next token preloaded.
Model: llama.cpp PR #20340 (fused sequential GDN kernel was faster than the chunk form there on Qwen3.5),
research/13-deltanet-chunked.md.

| 12,000 tokens, rocprofv3 | DeltaNet recurrence | GPU total | NLL |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| k_dn_recur (early state) | 1,161 ms | 8.99 s | 0.915200778 |
| k_dn_recur with preloading, 2 instead of 3 synchronizations | 997 ms | 8.96 s | 0.915200778 |
| first version k_dn_wave (52 B scratch: loop not unrolled) | 3,427 ms | 11.31 s | 0.913882328 |
| **k_dn_wave** | **587 ms** | **8.55 s** | 0.913882328 |

Not bit-identical (different summation order), perplexity 2.494 instead of 2.497. Continuation after decode passes (open
tokens, server, 265 new tokens): answer equivalent, diverges after ~250 characters. `IE_DN_KERNEL=old` takes the
old kernel.

Assessment of the chunk form (WY, WMMA, research/13-deltanet-chunked.md): the recurrence is now at ~6.9 % of the GPU time; fp32 is at the
instruction throughput limit (≈ 410 instructions per token and wave, 6 waves per SIMD). The chunk form would save an estimated ~0.4 s
(~5 %), but needs two new kernels with their own numerics. Deferred; the element-wise kernels (k_combine,
k_mix, k_norm16: together ~1 s) are the cheaper next lever.
