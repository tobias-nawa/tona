## Plan 1: the measure (goal 1)

- **Scope:** the 25 stored Claude Code turns of `2026-11-10-fp8-stop.md` (prompt 32K to 70K tokens, 165 to 3,183 turn
  tokens, 25 turns of the stored sessions), re-rendered with the current `tona-chat --anthropic` (mid-history system

  `TONA_DETERMINISTIC=1`. 50 runs, about 1.5 min each (75 min).
- **Statistic:** dNLL = NLL(FP8) minus NLL(f16) per turn token, mean over all tokens with standard error
  (sd / sqrt n; tokens of one turn are correlated, so the se is a lower bound: unchecked, the per-turn means are
  given too), top-1 agreement of the two arms, and a truncated KL(f16 || FP8) over the top-50 tokens of the f16 arm
  (the dump keeps no full distribution; lower bound of the KL).
- **Expectation:** dNLL +0.02 nat/token (se 0.003) as in `2026-11-10-fp8-stop.md` Result 4 (it was measured with the
  old prompt construction; the turn tokens are the same, only the context changes). Tolerance +-0.005. A value
  outside is a finding.
- **Active tooling:** `TONA_DETERMINISTIC=1`, `TONA_TEACHER_SPECIAL=1`, `TONA_TEACHER_DUMP`/`TONA_TEACHER_NLL`; no
  diagnostics.

## Result 1 (14:55 to 17:00 on 11-10, resumed after a pause): the measure

25 turns, 21,066 turn tokens, FP8 on against off (same procedure as Plan 1, 50 runs, 75 s each):
**dNLL +0.0150 nat/token, se 0.0017 over tokens, 0.0027 over the 25 per-turn means** (the second is the honest one,
tokens within a turn are correlated). Top-1 agreement 0.939, truncated KL(f16 || FP8) 0.031 (top-50 of the f16 arm;
lower bound). Per turn -0.024 to +0.047. Expectation was +0.02 +-0.005: the value is lower than the old run (+0.021 over
5,355 tokens, +0.02 over 25 turns with the old prompt construction) but still above 0.01 by about 1.9 per-turn se.

`TONA_PF_FP8_STATS=1` on one 33K turn: relative error of the FP8 copy of the input 2.4 to 2.6 % in every GEMM shape,
flushed to zero below 0.03 %, crest factor up to 50. The factor per token is max/448, so saturation cannot occur
by construction; the error is the plain rounding noise of 3 mantissa bits (rms 2^-4/sqrt(3) relative), no outlier-channel
problem on the activation side. The weight side has the same 3 bits plus the per-row factor (unmeasured).
