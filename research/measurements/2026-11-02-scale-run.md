## Plan

Why now: the scale run is due (every three days, `docs/contribute/measurement.md`), and the branch `86-host-memory` changes the host memory
(GTT by `HSA_USERPTR_FOR_PAGED_MEM=0`, the experts that do not fit the GTT registered as userptr). At 1M the share that does not fit
grows (1.2 GB at 140K, 4.7 GB at 262K), so 1M is where the change can hurt. One run covers R7 and the scale run for this change; the
other scale items (`set`, `mid`/`long` curve, needle at 128K) are not repeated here: `mid` and `long` and `ctx` ran in the #86 acceptance.

1. Scope
   - `scripts/ab.py --a merged --b w83 --workload huge` (`merged` = main 9aaf6e9, `w83` = this branch rebased on it): 512K and 1M prompt
     (summarize code), 200 decode tokens, `--ctx 1048576`, `IE_SEQS=1`, MTP. No entry for `huge` in `bench/noise.toml`: 2 rounds
     (`--min-rounds 2 --max-rounds 2`), AB then BA, `--no-warmup` (a discarded warm-up start of A would cost 23 min; the order
     alternates, so a first-start effect cancels in the pairs). One start prefills 512K (343 s) and 1M (911 s) and decodes 200 tokens:
     about 23 min; four starts: about 1 h 35 min.
   - Needle, R7: `bench/needle/run.py --hard --length 1048576 --texts 0`, one text per arm, in a server of its own per arm
     (`IE_SEQS=1 IE_PROMPT_RAM_GB=0 --ctx 1048576`, `MemoryMax=54G`, `MemorySwapMax=0`, port 8097). Prefill 1M about 17 min plus 40
     questions: about 20 min per arm. Pool `/var/tmp/claude/w77b/pool.txt` (the pool of the long-context phase 1).
   - Total GPU time about 2 h 15 min, under the GPU lock (`ab.py` takes it itself; the needle script runs under `flock`).
   - Why this size: at 1M the pinned memory, the GTT use and the registered share are largest, and the prefill rate at 512K and
     1M is where the earlier long-context notes have numbers. Smaller prompts or `ctx 262144` do not reach the case.
2. Expectation (B against A; sources: `2026-10-31-long-context-topk.md` for 512K, `2026-10-31-long-context-yarn.md` section 2 for 1M,
   `2026-11-01-slow-decode.md` for the registered share)
   - Prefill 512K 1,434 tok/s, 1M about 1,030-1,080 tok/s (1,029 in the needle run, 1,080 for a 1,001,235-token conversation); B within
     -2..+1 % of A.
   - Decode 512K 60.5 tok/s (2.83 tokens per pass); 1M: no earlier figure, expected 50-60 tok/s; B at most 2 % below A.
   - Start log of B: `host memory: GTT`; fixed host buffers (KV int8 14.5 GB in RAM at 1M, staging) about 17-18 GB, which fits the GTT
     (32.5 GB); room for the pinned experts about 13-14 GB, so about 11 GB (9-13) of the 25.1 GB pinned experts registered as userptr
     (prediction from 1.2 GB at KV 1.9 GB and 4.7 GB at KV 3.6 GB, one sequence slot instead of two). If the fixed buffers alone do not
     fit, the start fails with the message that names `HSA_USERPTR_FOR_PAGED_MEM=1`: that is a finding, not a reason to rebuild.
   - 512K and 1M: server RSS about 45 GB (as in the yarn note) inside `MemoryMax` RAM minus 6 GiB; no abort, no fault, no oom-kill.
   - Expert counters (`/status`) identical in both arms; text SHA of A and B equal.
   - Needle, hard, 1M, one text: 39 % of 40 needles in the earlier `off` runs (about 16 hits); A and B within 4 hits of each other (one
     text: the needles are not independent, no test is applied).
3. Active tooling
   - `IE_DIAG=1` in both arms (allocation trace per PID, GPU dump, core limit unlimited; cost measured in `2026-11-02-ab-tool.md` of
