## S1 plan: Mamba-2 prefill as chunked SSD on WMMA (decision of the coordinator: tolerance instead of bit-equality)

Ceiling and gap: per token and layer the scan has 4.2 MFLOP on the vector units (sequential form, 0.17 us at 24 TFLOPS float) and
2.8 us measured (dt 0.1 + scan 1.74 + the rest of the Mamba-2 kernels), 10x over the arithmetic bound (B4). The chunked form does
5.3 MFLOP per token and layer on the matrix units (chunk 64: scores C.B^T per group 0.06, intra-chunk M.Xdt 0.5, inter-chunk
C.S^T 1.0, state update 1.0 MFLOP per head and chunk of 64 tokens, summed over 128 heads and divided by 64), 93 TFLOPS f16 peak, 30 TFLOPS
as a working number: 0.18 us per token and layer. Design (`gpu/prefill_mamba_ssd.hip`): one block of 4 waves per head, the 64
rows of the head split over the waves, the chunks one after the other with the transposed state S^T (128 x 16 per wave) in the
accumulators; the scores C.B^T are computed once per (chunk, group) by a second kernel and shared by the 16 heads of a group;
operands f16, accumulators and the state f32. Chunk grid = absolute position / 64.

Tolerance, stated before the first run (like the Coder's prefill golden): normed output (f16) and state at most 3e-3 relative to the
largest value against the oracle (the sequential scan reaches 4.7e-4 and 1.4e-7: f16 operands carry 5e-4 relative each, a product of
two 1e-3 per term, the sums average that down, the bound is about twice that). Cut invariance: a prompt cut at multiples of 64 is
bit-equal to the whole; a prompt cut anywhere stays within 3e-3 of the whole; a decode pass after a chunked prefill stays within 3e-3
of the decode pass after the sequential prefill. Then at the model level (below, S2): decode after a kernel-prefilled prompt vs
decode-prefilled (as in B6), N2 at 128 / 4K / 32K tokens with the oracle where affordable, N7 against llama.cpp-hip.

| Question | Outcomes | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Is the chunked kernel within the stated tolerance and cut-invariant on the grid? | Yes: go on to the speed. Output or state above 3e-3: find the cause (operand rounding vs a bug: compare against a double reference of the same chunked formulas) before widening the bound | `test_gpu_mamba` (unit test, GPU, no model): checks listed above |
| Speed of dt + scan per token and layer, 4096 tokens | At or below 0.5 us (3.5x better than 1.84 us): keep as default (`TONA_MAMBA_SSD=0` gives the sequential form back). Above 1.0 us: the kernel is not doing what the arithmetic says, profile with counters before tuning | same test, the existing timing block of the test (3 repetitions, spread from the repetitions) |

Tooling: none besides the test binary. Expected duration: seconds. Run as part of one `gpu` job (it times nothing that decides a
