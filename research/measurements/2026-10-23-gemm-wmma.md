# f16 GEMM with WMMA, first kernel (prefill, phase 3)

Own measurement, 2026-10-23, ~20:00. `bench/micro/gemm.hip`, Y[T×N] = X[T×K] · W[N×K]ᵀ, f16 in, f32 out.
Tile 128 × 128 per block (8 waves, 64 × 32 each), K step 32, LDS double-buffered, prefetch into registers.
Per shape 3 × 20 calls, best value and range. Error: largest relative deviation of 512 samples against double.

TFLOPS, range within 3 runs up to −20 % on the first run (clock). Error ≤ 1.7e-4.

- Without reordering the blocks (output tile fastest) only 19–22 TFLOPS for the wide shapes: every row
  of blocks re-read W (52–63 MB), more than L2 and Infinity Cache. With groups of 8 token tiles: 41–52.
- Ceiling: measured 93 TFLOPS (WMMA f16, measurements/2026-10-22-micro), we are at 40–55 %.
  At ~13 GFLOP per token, 40 TFLOPS is enough on paper for ~3,000 tok/s (prefill).
- Next steps on the kernel (later): larger wave tile (64 × 64), K step 64, experts with few tokens
  (at 8192 tokens and top-10 of 256, an expert gets ~320 tokens per layer).
