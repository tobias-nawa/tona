# Effort levels and thinking loops

Own measurement, 2026-10-26. `scripts/bench-effort.py`, 23 own Python tasks with tests,
ie-server with MTP, `--ctx 16384`, `max_tokens` 24000 (in practice limited by the context).
Raw data: `work/effort/2026-10-26-1226.jsonl`, `-1327.jsonl`, `-1348.jsonl`.

## Run 1: levels, greedy (like Claude Code: no temperature)

| Level | passed | thinking tokens median | total time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| off | 21/23 | 0 | 86 s |
| low | 18/23 | 1227 | 1077 s |
| medium | 22/23 | 952 | 494 s |

Almost all failures: thinking until the context is full, no code block.

## Run 2: the 8 outliers of "high", greedy against sampling

Sampling = Qwen's recommendation for thinking: T 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 20, seed 1.

| Task | greedy | thinking tok. | decode tok/s | Sampling | thinking tok. | decode tok/s |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|

| csv | loop | 16220 | 115.5 | ok | 9317 | 31.2 |

- Greedy: 6 of 8 are literal repetitions ("Potential issue: from_roman('XVX') okay." …),
  checked at the end of the thinking block (`think_tail`). Loops run fast (100–117 tok/s decode)
  because the MTP drafts then almost always hit.
- Sampling: not a single loop. Two tasks think too long, but with changing content.
- **Cost:** decode with sampling ~30 tok/s (scatter 28.7–31.9), greedy without a loop ~70 tok/s.
  Cause still open (hits of the drafts under sampling, or sampling itself).

## Conclusion

Greedy decoding with thinking leads to loops with this model; this matches the warning
on Qwen's model card. Sampling during thinking fixes the loops; a thinking budget catches the brooding.

## Addendum: sampling brake found

At T 1, `make_dist` sorted the whole vocabulary (152K candidates in the 30·T window): 7.8 ms per position,
`partial_sort` to top_k 20 0.5 ms (microbenchmark, 2 runs equal). Per pass a distribution is built for every verified
position. After the change, sampling as above, high:

| Task | Thinking tok. | Decode tok/s before | after | Tokens per pass |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| flatten | 13181 | 30.4 | **53.6** | 2.07 |
| weekday | 10982 | 31.9 | **60.5** | 2.53 |

The texts are not the same as in the first run: without `IE_DETERMINISTIC` the logits depend on the pass width,
and the number of random draws on the drafts. The selection rule is unchanged. Passed here 1/2 (flatten ok,
weekday wrong date); two runs say nothing about quality.

Next lever: the MTP draft is always the argmax (q = one-hot), acceptance is then with p(draft). If the
draft is drawn from its own distribution, acceptance rises to Σ min(p, q) (Leviathan 2211.17192).

## Server default sampling and MTP draft from q

From now on without a temperature in the request: T 1.0 / top_p 0.95 / top_k 20 (with thinking), seed random. The MTP draft
is drawn from its own distribution q (logits of the head, same temperature, top_k, top_p), acceptance with min(1, p/q),
otherwise residual max(0, p − q). Same 8 tasks, high, one server start each:

| | passed | thinking tok. median | total decode | decode per task | Tokens per pass |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| draft greedy (`IE_MTP_SAMPLE=0`) | 6/8 | 9296 | 55.5 tok/s | 52.5–59.5 | 2.06–2.52 |
| **draft from q** | 6/8 | 7438 | **64.5 tok/s** | 60.3–67.7 | 2.61–3.13 |

- +16 % decode, with the same distribution of the output (lossless); the passed count varies with the seed.
- No loop in either run (end of the thinking blocks checked); greedy it was 6 of 8.
- Cost: logits of the head per draft to the host (shortened head) and a distribution over top_k on the CPU.
- Once before (first run with the new default, still with a greedy draft): abort "barrier aborted
  (timeout)", host.wait 1000 ms at position 5436. The protection circuit kicked in, no hang; not again afterwards in 16
  tasks.

## medium against high with sampling (all 23 tasks, server default, draft from q)

`work/effort/2026-10-26-1612.jsonl`. Between 16:30 and 16:36 a build ran twice: speed of these tasks not
usable, passed count and thinking tokens are.

| Level | passed | thinking tok. median | P90 | max | over 8,000 | total time | loops |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| medium | **21/23** | **851** | 2,667 | 5,299 | 0 | **477 s** | 0 |
| high | 20/23 | 5,591 | 16,224 | 16,248 | 8 | 2,276 s | 0 |

- medium: two wrong solutions (roman, weekday). high: three tasks brooded until the end of the context (roman, expr, edit).
- high takes 4.8× as long and thinks more than 8,000 tokens on 8 of 23 tasks; with `CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS=8192`
  in `just claude` no answer would arrive there.
- Conclusion: medium as the default for agents; high only deliberately.
