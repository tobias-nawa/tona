### Design decisions (open items for the coordinator are marked OPEN)

- **Decode steps.** Three steps around the ordinary in/out matvecs: conv (ring of K-1+BMAX positions, no commit needed:
  positions past a rollback are overwritten), scan (a wave per head and 8 rows, state 128 over the lanes, the replay
  like DeltaNet: the stored state lags one pass, the pass first commits the accepted tokens from stored decay, x*dt
  and B), gated group norm with int8. The NVFP4 tensor scales are folded into the reads of the in-projection output and
  into the norm output (the matvec is linear), 1 for other types.
- **Prefill scan is sequential in time, not the chunked SSD matrix form (OPEN).** The ticket text says chunked SSD. The
  matrix form (Dao and Gu, 2405.21060) reorders the sums per chunk, so a prompt cut at another place would end in
  different bits; N4 asks for one piece against random pieces to be bit-equal, and the decode path continues a
  prefilled state. The sequential form shares every line of arithmetic with the decode step (`gpu/mamba_dev.h`), so it
  is bit-equal by construction. Cost: 4.2 MFLOP per token and layer on the vector units against about 5.4 MFLOP of the
  matrix form (counted for L = 64, Bolt shapes) that run on WMMA; N3 measures whether the sequential form is
  within 2x of its bound. The chunked form stays an option if N3 says no, then with its tolerance stated instead of
  bit-equality. This is the coordinator's decision.
- **State** through `Mega::alloc_state` (`gpu/arch/nemotron_h/state.cpp`): reset, snapshots, stash, prompt cache and
  slots need no knowledge of Mamba. 5.0 MB per layer (4 MiB SSM state), 200 MB for 40 layers.

### Expectation (tolerance, source)

- Step 0: identical machine code (the diff moves host code only), identical tokens.
- Decode against the oracle: relative to the largest value at most 2e-3 (float32 state, float32 conv; the oracle
  accumulates the dot in double; source: Ornith/Coder DeltaNet tests at similar size). Prefill output as f16: 5e-3.
- Replay and cuts: bit-equal, no tolerance.
- N3 bound for decode: state read and write 4 MiB x 40 layers = 335 MB per pass at about 320 GB/s: 1.0 ms per pass at
  most (the commit write happens only after an accepted pass). Prefill: scan 40 layers x 8192 rows x 128 x 4 flop = 168
  MFLOP per token on 24 TFLOPS float: 7 us per token; GEMMs of Bolt dominate (4.8 GB of weights per token).

## Prior art

Change type: `technique` (new kernels for a new architecture), `refactor` for step 0.

- [Dao and Gu, Transformers are SSMs (SSD), arXiv 2405.21060](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.21060), status: read. The
  recurrence is the oracle's form; the chunked matrix form is the alternative for the prefill scan (see OPEN above).
- [ReplaySSM: Cache SSM Inputs, Not State](https://tridao.me/blog/2026/replayssm/), status: read (blog). Adopted: cache the
  inputs of the last pass and apply the accepted prefix when the next pass starts (the design of the Coder's DeltaNet,
  arXiv 2610.13778 in `mega.h`), here with decay, x*dt and B of Mamba-2. Not adopted: output directly from the cache
  without writing the state back (our state write happens once per pass at the commit).
- [SpecMamba, arXiv 2509.19873](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19873) and
  [Mamba drafters, arXiv 2506.01206](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01206), status: skimmed. Backtracking problem statement;
  not adopted (FPGA, other drafting scheme).
- llama.cpp `ssm_scan` (CPU, CUDA, Metal; Metal change #14743: parallel over d_state, about 3x prefill): sequential
  over tokens with the state in registers; the form adopted here for the scan, the same lane mapping over d_state.
  Not found: whether its CUDA kernel has a chunked form (no source found).
- Modular's Mojo `mamba2_ssd_scan`: first GPU version sequential, chunked tiling as a later performance slice; the same
  order of work as here.

Search: two web searches by hand (rollback of SSM state in speculative decoding; llama.cpp ssm_scan and chunked SSD
kernels), window 2024 to 2026; `tools/papers/scan.py` not run, open item before the pull request text.

**Changes to the plan.** (1) The first kernels were slower than the plan allowed (N3, see below); three optimizations were
made and measured one at a time (reduction over DPP instead of ds_bpermute, one block per group in the decode norm, dt and
decay computed once per token and head in the prefill, 64-bit divisions out of the conv). (2) Step 0's goldens run once on the
final tree instead of once per step: the B4 change touches no Coder file (C1 below), so identical goldens on the final tree
cover step 0 too.

**Step 0 (own commit `1715f02`).** C1 `isa-diff` of the shared baseline build of main `52c944b` (queue job of the B5 worker, read
only) against the step-0 build: 378 functions in A and B, all identical. Ornith prefill (`tona-logits-dump --prefill`, 1,648
ids, 16 last positions, `TONA_DETERMINISTIC=1`): base and step-0 logits byte-identical (`cmp`). Coder goldens: see below.
What the shared driver still carries that is Coder-shaped and was not asked for (open, no change made): `PfBufs::inj_a/inj_f/

**N4 replay.** Three seeds, 15 to 18 passes of random width 1 to 8 with random accepted counts and re-fed tokens, 48 tokens:
outputs of all accepted tokens and the final state bit-equal to passes of exactly the accepted tokens. Prefill: 130 tokens in
one piece against random pieces (1 to 40 tokens): outputs and state bit-equal; state after decode-only equals the prefilled
state bit for bit; a 4-token decode pass after the prefilled state equals the all-decode run bit for bit.

**N2 prefill.** 128 and 4096 tokens: output (f16) worst 4.7e-4 and 4.8e-4 (f16 rounding), state 7.3e-8 and 1.4e-7.

**N1 (`kernel-regs.py`).** The probe megakernel with the Mamba profile: 192 VGPRs at all widths (the limit), 107 SGPRs, VGPR
spills 19 / 97 / 82 and scratch 288 / 564 / 544 bytes at widths 1 / 4 / 8. For comparison Ornith's production kernel has 40 /
54 / 70 VGPR spills (DeltaNet in it), the plain-residual probe 0. LDS is not larger than the kernel's (the steps use no LDS
beyond `sm`). The Mamba scan raises the spills to the DeltaNet level; moving the token step into its own function (needed for the
function-size gate) raised the width-4 spills from 64 to 97, a tuning item for B6 (the profile of the Bolt kernel decides).
Prefill kernels: 14 to 104 VGPRs, no spills.

**N3 decode.** `Rig::time_us` includes a fixed cost per launch of about 82 us (the conv step alone, which does about nothing,
shows it), so the cost of a step is the difference. Per layer, 3 steps and 2 barriers: width 1 about 29 us, width 4 about 59 us,
width 8 about 86 us above that fixed cost; the scan alone 17, 38, 64 us (commit of nb tokens included). Bound: state read and write
4 MiB + 4 MiB, 8.4 MB at 320 GB/s = 26 us (the state also fits the 32 MB Infinity Cache, so the real bound is lower, the scan at
width 1 moves 470 GB/s of it). Width 1: within 2x (1.1x). Widths 4 and 8: 2.3x and 3.3x; the tokens of a pass are processed one
after the other in registers, so the cost grows with the width. Finding, not tuned here.

**N3 prefill.** 4096 tokens per call, per token and layer: dt 0.1 + scan 1.74, conv 0.60, norm 0.36, ring 0.01: 2.82 us in total
(from 6.7 us before the optimizations), 113 us per token for 40 layers = 8,900 tokens/s for the Mamba layers alone; the GEMMs of
Bolt (4.8 GB of weights per token) bound the model at about 5,000 tokens/s, so this is about 40 % on top. Bound of the scan:
4.2 MFLOP per token and layer on the vector units (0.17 us) or the memory floor of the chain 0.8 us: the scan is 2.2x of the
memory floor and 10x of the vector-FLOP bound. Cause measured: instruction-bound (about 230 VALU instructions per token and
wave after the optimizations, 500 before; 16 waves per SIMD), not latency (the shuffles through DPP gained 3 %). **N3 prefill
outcome: not within 2x of the arithmetic bound.** The next step is the chunked matrix form on WMMA (the SSD paper), which moves
the arithmetic to the matrix units, at the price of the bit-equality across cuts. That is the coordinator's decision (OPEN above);
the ticket's text names the chunked form, this implementation does not have it.
