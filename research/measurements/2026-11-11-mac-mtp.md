# Mac: the MTP head on Metal, checked against the reference, and its cut to 164 experts (#108)

M1 Max 32 GB, Metal backend (`0a73787` and later on `108-mac-m2`), target DM (the Coder cut to 164 experts per
layer, byte copy, `work/models/coder-dmix/`), MTP head `mtp-Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Q8_0.gguf` (512 experts, Q8_0).

## Plan (written before the runs)

- Golden comparison: `tona-check DM "first 2000 bytes of scripts/bench-long-code.txt" --mtp HEAD --golden FILE`
  against the reference's dumps (same prompt, `-n 64`, 3 steps, 256 calls); expectation: top-1 equal in most calls,
  logits close; the reference rounds activations to int8, so not bit-equal. Tolerance set from the first comparison.
- Routing statistics: tona-server DM with the full head, `TONA_MTP_ROUTE_STATS`, `TONA_PREFILL_CHUNK=512` (with 2048
  the working set overflows, below), load `bench/mac/mtp_route_load.py` (12 tasks x 12 languages x 2 styles, greedy, up
  to 400 tokens) until 50K decode tokens; expected ~35 min at ~24 tok/s. Keep the 164 experts with the largest routing
  mass; stability: the keep sets of the two halves of the entries and the mass of each half on the whole's keep set.
  Expected: well above 80 % of the mass on 164 of 512 (the target's cut kept 90 % on average), halves within a few
  points.
- Speed is not claimed here (server end to end: 2026-11-12-mac-mtp-server.md).

Test conditions (`tools/hw_report.py`, 23:18): Apple M1 Max, 10 cores, 32.0 GiB; macOS 27.0.1 (arm64); no `TONA_*`
in the shell; load average 1.35; other programs 5.5 % of all threads; quiet check ok. Before the statistics run
(22:50) the quiet check warned (load average 2.61 from the builds before it); after it (23:14) "do not measure: the
GPU is 96 %", the end of the run itself. Speed does not enter these results. Swap: ~17 GB in use before and during the
runs (system load before the session's runs), growth during a run at most 0.3 GB.

## Golden comparison

| Golden (reference, hip-gfx1200) | Calls | Top-1 equal | Relative deviation of the golden top 5 (median / p90 / max) |
|---|---|---|---|
| DM, MTP experts in Q8_0 (`TONA_MTP_EXP=q8`, the same arithmetic as Metal) | 256 | **241** | 5.8e-2 / 1.24e-1 / 4.2e-1 |
| mini model, Q8_0 | 256 | 0 | - |

- DM: 14 of the 15 differing calls have Metal's pick in the reference's top 5, at golden gaps of 0.18 to 4.5 between
  first and second against a median gap of 7.8 over all calls: near ties, not a structural difference. Proposals that
  hit the next token of the golden sequence: reference 58 / 50 / 44 / 37, Metal 57 / 49 / 40 / 36 (fill, steps 1 to
  3; 189 against 182 of 246).
- **Tolerance** (`tona-check --golden`, from this comparison): top-1 equal in at least 90 % of the calls and a median
  relative deviation of at most 0.1. Bit-equality is out of reach: the reference rounds the activations of every
  mat-vec to int8, Metal computes in f32, and the difference of the target reaches the head through h.
- **Finding, mini model:** the reference's MTP logits run to 3e8 to 1.7e9 at token ids 247924 to 247980 with one NaN
  (also with Q8_0 experts; the reference on the real Coder gives a normal max of 23.4: reference ticket #119, f16
  overflow suspected, unchecked). Metal's logits stay at 27 to 41. Over the full rows of the first 8 calls without
  ids >= 247924 the argmax of both is equal in 8 of 8; the mini model is no yardstick for the head.

## Paging with the full head

Server DM + full head, `--ctx 8192`, 3 requests of 200 tokens each (diagnosis, not a speed claim):

| Prefill chunk | Server's resident estimate | Decode tok/s | Page-ins per request (16 KB) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2048 | 26.95 GB | 8.7 / 5.4 / 7.6 | 167K / 200K / 153K |
| 512 | 26.06 GB | 24.5 / 21.5 / 27.1 | 15.8K / 9.6K / 3.7K |

The prefill scratch of the 2048 chunk pushes the working set past the 26.8 GB of `recommendedMaxWorkingSetSize`; the
GPU then pages the mapped weights. With the full head only a chunk of 512 fits.
