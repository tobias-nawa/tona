**What counts as a finding for the FP8 needle (set before the first run).** Needle outcomes are paired (same needle, both
arms). With a share psi of discordant needles of 0.25 (the usual share between two variants; `bench/needle/README.md`),
the difference of hits has a standard deviation of sqrt(psi n): 3.2 hits for one text (n = 40), 5.5 hits for the sum of
three texts (n = 120). A finding is
- the sum over the three 128K texts differs by 11 hits or more (about two standard deviations, 9 points), or `report.py
  compare` gives McNemar p < 0.05 on the 120 pairs, or
- one text differs by 7 hits or more (two standard deviations of one text, the weaker criterion; it then needs the other
  texts to point the same way before it is called systematic),
- at 1M (one text, 40 pairs): a difference of 7 hits or more, or a collapse below 10 of 40 in either arm.

Smaller differences are reported as "not distinguishable at this n", never as "equal". The direction of FP8 against off is
reported either way. For h3 the finding is a different token count or SHA (expected, S2) and the text of both answers is read
and compared on the task (a bullet-list summary).

A number outside its range is a finding (a line in the results, an issue by the coordinator), not noise.

## Correction 2026-11-11

The R8 evicted time was evaluated from the field `evicted_ms` of `r8.jsonl` (the counter change over the request window
of the sampler, mostly 0) instead of `evicted_request` (the evicted time attributed to the request). With the right
field the mean per pressured request is 2.08 s (1.10 to 4.22 s), not 0.05 s. #103 did not go away; the issue comment
of 2026-11-06 and the BENCHMARKS row are corrected.
