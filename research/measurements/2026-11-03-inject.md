# gr.down: take the inject off the critical path, bit-identical (#37)

Date: 2026-11-03. Branch `37-inject` (from main 3a70f42). Builds `work/build/w37inj` (this branch) and
`work/build/w37inj-base` (main, the other side of A/B).

## Plan: step profile of the placement variants (block 1)

Question: does a different placement of the units of `gr.down` (320 rows Q8_0) and of its inject (4 rows x 10,240 BF16)
on the waves and blocks of the megakernel shorten the step and the pass at B = 1? Every unit keeps its own chain of
sums, so the results are bit-identical by construction; only the unit-to-wave mapping changes
(`DevTask::skip`, `DevTask::spread`, knobs `IE_GR_SPREAD` and `IE_GR_GAP`, temporary).

Variants (what the knob does):
- V0 base: `IE_GR_SPREAD=0 IE_GR_GAP=0` (today: down units on waves 0..319 = blocks 0..19, inject units from wave 320 on
  blocks 20/21, active units 320, 328, 336, 344 at nb = 1).
- V1 `IE_GR_SPREAD=3`: down and inject units go round robin over the 32 blocks (10 down waves per block, inject on
  wave 10 of blocks 0, 8, 16, 24).
- V2 `IE_GR_GAP=32` (spread 0): the inject starts at wave 352, so its active units sit on blocks 22 and 23, which have no
  down work today.
- V3 `IE_GR_SPREAD=1`: only the down units spread, the inject stays at wave 320 (the same waves as 10 of the spread down units;
  expected to be slower, control).

Scope: `ie-gpu-gen --mega`, coder IQ1_M, prompt csv of `scripts/bench-decode.sh` in the chat format, 200 tokens, no
drafter (every pass B = 1), as in 2026-11-03-hc-splitk.md. `IE_MEGA_PROF=1`; two runs for V0 and V1, one for V2 and V3;
then one run per variant with `IE_BAR_PROF` (per-block times with `scripts/barprof.py`). 60 s pause between runs,
all under `/var/tmp/claude/gpu.lock`, `systemd-run` with `MemoryMax=44G`, no build during the runs. ~1.5 min per run,
~25 min in total.

Why this size: the effect is at most 0.24 ms of 23.56 ms (1 %), the step profile of one pass kind is deterministic
within 0.1 ms (23.85 / 23.71 on 11-02); the profile is the cheapest way to see the step time before the decode A/B
(`ab.py`) is worth its GPU hours. Abort rule of the ticket: a gain under 0.08 ms per pass ends the work.

Expectation (source: hc-splitk.md, "Reading"): V0 pass 23.56 +- 0.1 ms, gr.down 1,875 +- 20 us per pass, blocks 0..3
17.5, 4..15 15.0, 16..19 17.9, 20/21 17.1, 22..31 7.9 us. The ceiling is 2.5 us per call x 97 = 0.24 ms.
For V1 I expect between 0 and -0.2 ms on gr.down (the 3.5 MB are read by 32 instead of 20 blocks, but the probe
reached the same ~215 GB/s with 32 blocks busy; **ungeprueft**). V2 about 0 (the down blocks stay the longest).
V3 worse than V0 (the inject waves also hold down units).

Active tooling: `IE_MEGA_PROF=1`, `IE_BAR_PROF=<file>` (the bar runs only), `IE_DIAG=1`, `IE_MEGA_INFO=1`,
`OMP_NUM_THREADS=6`, `IE_EXPERT_PROFILE`.

## Results: step profile (block 1), no variant is faster: discarded

Runs of the plan above (`prof-*.log`, `bar-*.bin` in `/var/tmp/claude/w37inj/`), build `work/build/w37inj`, B = 1, csv,
200 tokens, step profile on. Several runs waited long for the GPU lock (the other worker); no build ran during a run.
Mini model first: `golden.sh compare` (GOLDEN_MODEL=mini) identical to the references for spread 0, 1, 3 and spread 3
with gap 40.

| Variant | runs | pass ms (mean over tokens) | gr.down us per pass | gr.up us per pass | decode tok/s (B = 1) |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| V0 base | 2 + BAR | 23.76, 23.78 (BAR 23.89) | 1,880, 1,878 (1,887) | 1,691, 1,701 | 41.33, 41.69 |
| V1 spread 3 | 2 + BAR | 23.78, 23.82 (BAR 23.95) | 1,930, 1,929 (1,941) | 1,684, 1,697 | 41.70, 41.65 |
| V2 gap 32 | 1 + BAR | 23.76 (BAR 23.90) | 1,879 (1,884) | 1,695 | 41.70 |
| V3 spread 1 | 1 + BAR | 24.06 (BAR 24.20) | 2,193 (2,202) | 1,688 | 41.22 |

V0 is inside the expectation (23.56 +- 0.1 expected, 23.77 measured, +0.2 ms; gr.down 1,879 against 1,875). V1: gr.down
**+50 us**, pass +0.02 to +0.06 ms (worse or equal); V2: identical to V0; V3: +0.3 ms as expected. Gain of any variant
is below the abort limit (0.08 ms): **discarded**.

Per block, us per step of gr.down (barprof):
- V0: blocks 0 to 3 17.7, 4 to 15 15.0, 16 to 19 18.0, 20/21 17.3, 22 to 31 9.4.
- V1 (all blocks): 17.3 to 18.7, nearly uniform; the step is longer than the 15.0 us of the best base blocks.
- V2: the inject now sits on blocks 22/23 (17.2 us); blocks 0 to 3 (17.7) and 16 to 19 (18.0) did not change.

Reading (from the three block tables): the inject is **not** what makes blocks 0 to 3 and 16 to 19 slow: moving it
to blocks 22/23 leaves them at 17.7 and 18.0 us. They hold only down rows, as blocks 4 to 15 do, which need 15.0 us.
Why those eight blocks are slower than the others is not known (**ungeprueft**; the data do not show whether it is the
start time after the previous barrier, the memory channels of their weight rows or the clock). Spreading the rows
over 32 blocks makes every block 17.5 us, so the step time is not set by the rows per block either; it looks tied to
the total bytes in flight (3.5 MB in about 17 us, as in hc-splitk.md), **ungeprueft**. The ticket's ceiling of 0.24 ms
assumed the inject as the cause; with the cause not the inject, this lever is closed.

Not run because of the abort rule: `ab.py`, the big-model goldens, `isa-diff`. The bit-identical claim rests on the
design (same chain per unit) and the mini goldens, not on a big-model comparison. Gates (`just check`) and
`ctest -L unit` for the committed experiment code: see the report.
