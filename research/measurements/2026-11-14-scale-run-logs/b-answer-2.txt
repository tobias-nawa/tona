### **Benchmarking Harness (`ibench`)**
*   **CLI Interface:** Provides commands to `run` measurement suites against a target server, `compare` two JSON reports, `proxy` requests to record traces, and `env` to snapshot system state.

*   **Metrics Collection:** Measures Time-To-First-Token (TTFT), prefill/decode throughput (client and server-side), inter-token latency (ITL), token counts, and system resource usage (VRAM, RAM, GPU power/temperature).
*   **Reporting:** Generates raw JSON data and human-readable Markdown reports with statistical summaries (median, min/max, stdev) and environment snapshots.
