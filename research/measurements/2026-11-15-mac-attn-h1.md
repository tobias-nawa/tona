# Mac: one query head per threadgroup in the QSA prefill attention

Issue #108. An agent's tool step (35 new tokens at about 7900 tokens of context) spends 215 ms of GPU time in the QSA
attention ([cache-hit analysis](2026-11-15-mac-cache-hit.md)), against 29 ms for the same chunk at context 35. The
kernel `attend_t` runs 3 query heads per threadgroup (sharing the K and V loads of a window); a 35-token chunk gives
5 tiles × 2 KV heads × 4 head groups = 40 threadgroups on 32 GPU cores. `attend_t1` runs one head per threadgroup
(120 threadgroups, every K and V load 3 times), with the same bits (identical at the three references with
`TONA_METAL_ATTN_H1_BELOW` 0 and 1000000). A slice of a long prefill (512 tokens) gives 512 threadgroups with H = 3.

## Plan (written before the run)

**Decision.** Whether H = 1 gets a threshold by default (`TONA_METAL_ATTN_H1_BELOW`), and which. Possible results:
- (a) all-H1 lowers the step's attention by at least 20 ms and the 7722-token prefill is not slower: threshold above
  the step's grid (e.g. 1000000 or 600), then an `ab.py` A/B (prefill@8000, decode) and the agent steps.
- (b) it lowers the step's attention but slows the long prefill: threshold between 40 and 512 (128), A/B as in (a).
- (c) less than 10 ms gained per step: stays a probe, the reason goes here.
- (d) any answer differs between the arms: a bug, stop and find it (the bits are meant identical).

**Runs.** Scratch harness (one server, DM-T + MTP-164, `--ctx 32768`, a 7722-token request, then 4 tool steps of 36
new tokens, 0.5 s between them, `temperature` 0), `TONA_METAL_CB_LOG=1` (GPU ms by kind per prefill chunk);
arms `TONA_METAL_ATTN_H1_BELOW=0` and `=1000000`, order A B A B, about 4 min each, 16 min in all.
**Expectation.** Step attention 215 ± 10 ms in A; in B 100 to 180 ms (3 times the threadgroups on 32 cores, the
3-fold K/V reads cost ~10 ms at 400 GB/s); the long prefill's attention in B 0 to +30 % (its grid fills the GPU with
H = 3, so the extra loads show); answers of A and B equal (SHA of each step's answer).
**Active tooling.** `TONA_METAL_CB_LOG=1`, `TONA_GPU_GAPS=2` (as in the cache-hit runs); keepalive default (500 ms); otherwise defaults.

## Result

Four runs, order A B A B (lines per chunk in [the runs' log](2026-11-15-mac-attn-h1-runs.log), test conditions in
[the log](2026-11-15-mac-attn-h1.log)). GPU ms of the attention:

| Part | A (H = 3), run 1 / 2 | B (H = 1), run 1 / 2 | B vs A |
|---|---|---|---|
| tool step, 35 tokens (3 steps per run) | 214.6, 221.3, 221.1 / 214.0, 220.2, 220.0 | 146.2, 150.4, 151.1 / 146.2, 150.3, 150.6 | -70 ms (-32 %) |
| prefill chunk 2 of 4 (2048 tokens) | 2918.7 / 2860.1 | 2846.0 / 2837.8 | -2 % |
| prefill chunk 3 (2048) | 3966.1 / 3921.2 | 3813.6 / 3817.6 | -3 % |
| prefill chunk 4 (1577) | 3569.0 / 3528.9 | 3379.7 / 3370.0 | -5 % |
| tool step, prompt ms (`timings`) | 1054, 1001, 1001 / 1025, 998, 1022 | 960, 938, 972 / 971, 950, 927 | -55 ms (-5 %) |

Chunk 1 (no context yet) is equal (1270, 1261 against 1242, 1234). The answers are identical in all 4 runs and
all 5 requests (SHA of the 7722-token answer 727ff658ae742fa7, as in the A/Bs).

Against the plan: result (a), and better than expected: H = 1 is not slower on the long prefill either, its
attention is 2 to 5 % faster, so the shared K/V loads of H = 3 do not pay on this GPU even with a full grid (not checked
why: occupancy of the 3-head threadgroup's simdgroup matrices is the candidate). The step's attention fell to 148 ms,
inside the expected 100 to 180. The after check 30 s past the end reported the GPU 99 % busy and other programs
10.9 % of the threads (the same after-check reading as in the M1 A/B, with no server of ours running).

**Next.** H = 1 everywhere as the candidate default, `ab.py` A/B (prefill@8000 as the primary, decode unchanged
expected).

## A/B: H = 1 everywhere against the default

### Plan (written before the run)

**Decision.** Whether H = 1 becomes the default for all prefill chunks (then the template stays, `attend_t` with H = 3
goes, the variable goes). Possible results: prefill@8000 decided faster or open with a mean above 0 and decode not
decided slower → default (the agent step's -70 ms above is the main gain and the A/B cannot see it); prefill@8000
decided slower → finding against the runs above, no default until explained.
**Run.** `scripts/ab.py --workload set,server --targets 8000:100 --ctx 32768`, DM-T + MTP-164, the same build
(`work/build/mtl`, this branch after the merge of main) in both arms, B with `TONA_METAL_ATTN_H1_BELOW=1000000`,
2 to 6 rounds, margin 0.5 %, `--load-log on`, about 25 to 75 min.
**Expectation.** prefill@8000 +0.5 to +2 % (attention of the 7722-token prompt about 10.4 s of 43 s, -3.5 % of it is
-0.37 s, +0.9 %); decode-code, decode-text, decode@8000 -0.5 to +0.5 % (decode and MTP verification do not use
`attend_t`, only chunks on the GEMM path do).
**Active tooling.** The load log; defaults otherwise (keepalive 500 ms).

### Result

[Server A/B](2026-11-15-mac-attn-h1-server-ab.md), 6 rounds, test conditions in [the log](2026-11-15-mac-attn-h1.log):

| prefill@8000 | 162.8 | 167.9 | +1.89 % [-0.66, +4.44] | +0.5 to +2 % | open, within |
| decode@8000 | 25.2 | 25.1 | -0.27 % [-1.32, +0.77] | -0.5 to +0.5 % | open, within |

| decode-text | 27.2 | 27.1 | -0.60 % [-1.66, +0.45] | -0.5 to +0.5 % | open, **outside** |

Answers identical (SHA 727ff658ae742fa7, stable in both arms). Per the plan's rule (prefill open with a mean above 0,
decode not decided slower) H = 1 becomes the default: `attend_t` runs one head per threadgroup, the H = 3 kernel and
`TONA_METAL_ATTN_H1_BELOW` are gone. Identical at the three references and the MTP golden unchanged (241 of 256,
median 5.83e-02) with the default; unit tests 58 of 58.

**Finding: decode-text -0.60 %, outside the expectation.** Decode and MTP verification do not run `attend_t` (only
prefill chunks on the GEMM path do), so the decode passes of both arms execute the same kernels; the delta comes
mostly from round 4 (all three decode metrics -1.5 %), its interval contains 0. Not a change of the kernel; left open.
**Finding: the quiet check's GPU reading.** Before the A/B (15:47, no server of ours running since 15:34) it reported
the GPU 89 % busy and after it 98 %, 30 s later ok; the load log of the same starts shows other programs at 0.2 to
0.3 % of the GPU. The reading lags or counts something the load log does not; not checked.

Would it help the PC: no direct transfer (the HIP backend has its own attention kernels); the general point, one head
per workgroup when a chunk is short, is worth a look there.

**The GPU reading, checked (17:20).** `ioreg`'s "Device Utilization %" returns the value of the GPU's last work on
the first read after a pause and the current one from the next read on: 93 then 0, 0, 0 with no process of ours
running; 60 s after a short mini-model run 9 then 0, 0, 0. Not our process and not a command buffer left running: the
counter is stale on its first read. `tools/quiet_check.py` now reads twice, 1 s apart, and counts the second.
