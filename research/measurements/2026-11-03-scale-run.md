## Correction (coordinator, 2026-11-03, after the run)

Part 1 `huge` did **not** run with FP8 off. The coordinator rebuilt `work/build/merged` on 491bccb (FP8 on by default)
for the reference recording; that build took the GPU lock between `mid`/`long` and `huge` of part 1, so part 1 `huge`
loaded the FP8 binary. Shown by `2026-11-03-fp8-huge.md`: the 512K request with FP8 on reproduces `2b5e4d00485e53ae`
(both parts here), with `IE_PREFILL_FP8=0` it reproduces the 11-02 SHA `f7f5488a6a60d401`; FP8 gains **prefill**
+3.85 % at 512K (two paired runs) and about +3 % at 1M. Consequences for this file:

- c4 of part 1 is an FP8 measurement, so part 2 against part 1 at `huge` compares FP8 with FP8; finding 2 is withdrawn.
- The change of the `huge` SHAs since 11-02 (addendum of part 1) is FP8.
- The 1M answer of 26 tokens comes with FP8: without FP8 the same prompt gives 200 tokens (SHA `53cca9d9044bff02`, 74
  slots). The slot-count explanation was a guess and is withdrawn. Tracked in #104.
- All other blocks of part 1 (goldens, R4, `set`, `mid`/`long`) ran before the rebuild and stay FP8 off.
