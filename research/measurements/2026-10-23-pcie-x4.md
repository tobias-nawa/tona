# The GPU's PCIe link has run at only x4 since the 17:35 boot (2026-10-23, own measurement)

Noticed in the prefill profile: 26,403 host→GPU copies took 4.0 s in total.

- `gpubench pcie`, H2D pinned, 5 runs each: 64 KiB 7.2 · 256 KiB 11.6 · 1 MiB 13.5 · 16 MiB 14.3 · 1 GiB **14.3 GB/s**.
  On 2026-10-22 (measurements/2026-10-22-micro): **55.7 GB/s**.
- sysfs: GPU (03:00.0) and switch (02:00.0) x16 at 32 GT/s, but the link root port 00:01.1 ↔ switch upstream
  01:00.0 only **x4** (max x16).
- Kernel at the 17:35 boot: `pci 0000:03:00.0: 126.028 Gb/s available PCIe bandwidth, limited by 32.0 GT/s PCIe x4
  link at 0000:00:01.1 (capable of 504.112 Gb/s with 32.0 GT/s PCIe x16 link)`.
- The root port can do x16 (max_link_width 16), so not a split in the BIOS, but the connection trained at boot
  with only 4 lanes. Typical cause: card not fully seated in the slot (RAM upgrade today).
- Consequences: prefill copies of the pinned experts, decode on misses in the expert cache. All measurements from 17:35
  on (including prefill 744 tok/s) ran with x4.

Check after a reboot: `cat /sys/bus/pci/devices/0000:01:00.0/current_link_width` must show 16.

## Cause and fix (reboot 21:05)

- Intermediate state after the first reseating (20:54): x8, 28.8 GB/s.
- Cause: the screw at the bottom left that holds the mainboard (ROG STRIX B850-I, Mini-ITX) to the case kept the card
  from going all the way into the slot. Removing the screw fixed the seating.
- Afterwards: all links x16 at 32 GT/s, H2D pinned **55.6 GB/s** (256 KiB and 1 GiB, 5 runs each 55.53–55.61).
- Prefill 6,000 tokens (chunk 2,048): 582 (first run, model not yet in the page cache) / **969 / 969 tok/s (prefill)**,
  perplexity 3.187. With x4 it was 744.
